# Calm body and mouth — 2026-11-06

Branch `codex/body-calm`, on main after 1.0.0.

## Problem (owner)

On her own body form the eyes and face warp when she turns left or right, and a frown with half-open eyes is uncanny. Her mouth moves too fast and unnaturally.

## Cause (from the runtime's motion tracks)

- **Far head turns deform the face on the mesh:** `lookAround` ±20°, `no` ±13°, `shy` 14°, `glance` 13°, `tap`/`think` −12/−11°, `waiting` 12°, `lean` 10°, `spaceOut` −10°.
- **Half-closed eyes under a frown:** `sigh` (lids 0.6, brow −0.4), `stretch` (0.62, −0.3), `shy` (0.75, −0.6), `doze`, and the `halfLidded` (jito), `sad` and `angry` faces.
- **The runtime's random idle motions include all of these,** and they cannot be filtered.
- **The mouth:** her body used the runtime's default lip sync (fast syllables). The Live2D form set the mouth straight from 20 ms loudness every frame.

## Change

- **One calm filter at the body director's output** (`calm` in `packages/character/body.ts`). Those motions become `sway`, `tilt`, `giggle`, `nod` or `hum`, and those faces become neutral (angry becomes surprise), wherever they come from.
- **No runtime random idle motions** (`setAutoMotion(false)`). Her director plays a calm `sway` every 14 s instead.
- **Smaller ranges:** gaze ±0.35 (was ±0.55) and head yaw ±3.5° (was ±6°).
- **Mouth:** her body uses the runtime's Relaxed lip sync (granularity 0, close speed 0) at openness 0.4. The Live2D mouth is smoothed: it opens quickly and closes more slowly.

## Verification

- `tests/body.test.ts`:
  - a new test drives every phase for 200 s with pointer moves and typing, and asserts that no unsafe motion or face ever comes out;
  - a far pointer keeps yaw at most 3.5° and gaze at most 0.35;
  - existing tests are updated to the calm motions.
- `pnpm preflight`: 997 passed, 19 skipped. `pnpm build` and `pnpm test:scripted` pass.
- **Not verified by eye:** the motion and the mouth were not watched in a running app.
