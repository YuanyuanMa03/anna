# Commercial use

Anna's source is public, and it is free for noncommercial use under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Anything else needs a commercial license from the author.

## Free, no license needed

- Using Anna yourself: as a companion, for your own projects, for study, research, hobbies and experiments.
- Reading, learning from and modifying the code for those purposes, and sharing your changes noncommercially.
- Use by schools, public research institutions, charities and government bodies.

## Needs a commercial license

- Selling Anna, a modified Anna, or a product built on its code, including paid downloads, paid installers,
  bundles and app-store listings.
- Offering Anna or its code as part of a paid service.
- Using Anna inside a company or for client work.

If you are not sure which side your use falls on, ask.

license applies to the code after v0.4.1.
